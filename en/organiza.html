Virginia Molet & the Academy of Executive Coaching (AoEC)

The Academy of Executive Coaching (AoEC), founded in 1999, is an international organization specialized in leadership development and executive coaching. Over more than two decades, it has established itself as one of the global benchmarks in coach training and the integration of coaching within business contexts.

Their approach focuses on coaching not as a complementary skill, but as a form of leadership that directly impacts decision-making, team management, and organizational culture. Their programs, accredited by international bodies, combine methodological rigor with practical application in real corporate environments.

In Spain, the AoEC operates through its partner Virginia Molet, who leads the organization's development in the local market. From this position, she drives initiatives that connect executive coaching with the current challenges facing companies, contributing to raising the level of professionalization in the sector.
