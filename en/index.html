About the Organization

“The I International Executive Coaching Conference is an initiative born with the objective of connecting leadership development with the real challenges of organizations in an environment of increasing complexity.

This meeting brings together business leaders, experts in leadership development, and executive coaching professionals who are working on the front line of change in companies.

From an international perspective, the conference acts as a connection point between business practice, executive coaching, and the new dynamics driven by technology and artificial intelligence.

The initiative has the support of the Academy of Executive Coaching (AoEC) and is promoted in Spain by Virginia Molet.”
