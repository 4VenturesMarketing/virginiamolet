Privacy Policy

In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and organic laws on data protection, you are informed that the personal data provided through our website will be processed by the organization of the I International Executive Coaching Salon.

We process your information to manage your registration for the event, ensure your access to the facilities, and keep you informed about Salon news and future initiatives linked to executive development and corporate leadership.

2. Legal Basis

The legal basis for processing your data is the explicit consent you provide by accepting this policy and submitting your registration form.

Your data will be kept for the time necessary for event management and fulfillment of legal obligations, or until you request its deletion.

4. User Rights

You have the right to access, rectify, or delete your data, as well as the right to data portability and the limitation or opposition to its processing, by sending a request to the Salon organization.

5. Information Security

We apply technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, protecting your data against unauthorized access or accidental loss.
