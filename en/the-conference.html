I International Executive Coaching Conference

A pioneering initiative to gather professionals, organizations, and experts committed to leadership development, executive coaching, and talent evolution within the business landscape in Spain.

🎯 Origin & Drive

Driven by the Academy of Executive Coaching (AoEC) in Spain, the event brings together a carefully selected group of organizations, experts, and decision-makers who are defining how leadership and talent evolve within businesses.

  • ✓ Leadership development and executive training
  • ✓ Coaching applied to real corporate contexts
  • ✓ Technology and innovation in talent

✨ Conference Format

The inaugural edition will feature the participation of internationally renowned figures and will be structured as a dynamic environment combining different learning and networking methodologies:

  • 🎤 Keynotes from corporate leadership references
  • 💬 Strategic conversations with active business executives
  • 🤝 High-level networking spaces

The day centers around a critical question for organizations: how to develop leaders in an environment of growing complexity, transformation, and pressure for results.

This initiative is geared towards building a high-level ecosystem around executive coaching in Spain, elevating its role as a strategic tool for leadership development. An essential meeting point for HR Directors, Talent Managers, and Business Leaders.

The meeting point in Spain for leadership development, executive coaching, and talent evolution.
